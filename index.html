About Our School

Education, values, and discipline

Shri Balanand Swami Semi English Vidyalay, located in Waghunde Khurd, Maharashtra, is managed by Shri Bhairavnath Vidya Prasarak Sanstha. The school focuses on supporting students through education, positive values, discipline, and opportunities for learning and development.

We believe good education combines strong classroom learning with respect, regular habits, and the confidence to take part and ask questions.

  • An educational approach centred on steady academic learning.
  • Discipline and positive values as part of everyday school life.
  • A commitment to supporting every student's learning.
